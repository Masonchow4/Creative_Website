Fashion prices · BLS CPI-U · Apparel

Two cities.
One apparel index.

These charts use published BLS apparel CPI observations for New York–Newark–Jersey City and Dallas–Fort Worth–Arlington. The index shows price change over time; it is not a direct comparison of clothing price levels between cities.

Jan 2024 — Sep 2026Not seasonally adjustedAll Urban Consumers (CPI-U)Age group is not available in CPI
VISUALIZATION 01 / TREND

Apparel CPI over time

New York is reported monthly. Dallas is reported every two months, so its line connects the observations available at those intervals.

Hover or focus a point to see its date and index value. Missing October 2025 data in New York is left blank.

VISUALIZATION 02 / GROUP COMPARISON

Average index in matched periods

Both cities are compared only in the 17 periods where each has a published observation (January 2024–September 2026).

Most interesting pattern: New York’s apparel index averaged about 8.8 index points higher than Dallas’s across the matched dates. The two lines also move up and down over time, with Dallas observed less often.

How to read this: CPI area indexes measure changes relative to a base period and do not indicate that clothing is literally 8.8 points or a fixed amount more expensive in one city. This is a descriptive comparison, not a statistical significance test. CPI does not include an age 20–35 breakdown.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Public Data API v1. New York series · Dallas series Series: CUURS12ASAA · CUURS37ASAA
